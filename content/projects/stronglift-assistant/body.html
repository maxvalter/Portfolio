Background

I needed a bare-bones mobile app for logging workout progress and tracking development over time, keeping focus on my goals within the StrongLift 5×5 program.

Solution

The app runs as a mobile website. It stores all logged numbers in a database and suggests workout weights based on progress. You can attach videos to logs to review form, and view progress charts for each exercise. Built almost entirely with AI-assisted development in Cursor.
